# Defining genetic mechanisms of insulin resistance that shape fat partitioning and cardiometabolic disease risk

Repository to showcase the preliminary results for the project proposed for Danish Diabetes and Endocrine Academy (DDEA)'s postdoctoral fellowship of 2026.

## Aims:

The main aim of this project is to **identify genetically distinct mechanisms of IR that differentially influence fat distribution across adipose and ectopic depots and determine their clinical consequences and underlying molecular mechanisms.**

With these preliminary results **I aim to highlight to reviewers the strengths of our pipeline, providing confidence in the results that we can find and that those can be achieved in the two-year time.**

Particularly, these preliminary results will partially answer the following specific aims: 

1) Define distinct genetic mechanisms of IR through their effects on fat distribution and assess their clinical consequences.
2) Identify the genes, tissues, depots and cell types underlying distinct mechanisms of IR.
